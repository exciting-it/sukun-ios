### Description
Sukun shows the five daily prayers and the Qibla direction, quietly and accurately, where you look most: on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

MADE TO BE GLANCED AT
• Widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy
• The prayer whose time is running, with a countdown to the next, flipping exactly at each prayer time
• A large widget with all prayer times, the Hijri date and the Qibla bearing
• Widgets follow you when you travel

CALCULATED ON YOUR IPHONE
• All times are calculated on the device with the open-source Adhan library, so Sukun works offline and on a plane
• Picks the right method for your region automatically: Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, Diyanet, ISNA, Karachi and more
• Hanafi or standard Asr, and dedicated rules for high latitudes such as Northern Europe
• Adjust each time by a few minutes to match your mosque

QIBLA COMPASS
• Turn until the needle meets the mark; a soft haptic tells you when you’re aligned
• Uses true north when location is allowed, with a calibration hint when needed
• Shows the bearing and the distance to the Kaaba

GENTLE REMINDERS
• Optional local notifications at the start of each prayer, per prayer
• A reminder a few minutes before, and a Friday reminder for Jumu'ah

RAMADAN
• Turns on by itself: Suhoor and Iftar countdowns in the app and widgets
• Optional Suhoor reminder; Hijri date correction if Ramadan starts a day earlier or later where you live

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your location stays on your iPhone
• Prefer not to share your location? Choose your city instead

Available in English, German and Arabic. Supports Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Voice Control.
