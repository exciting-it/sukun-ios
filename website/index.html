Prayer times and the Qibla for iPhone. Quiet, accurate, and on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Coming soon to the App Store

Made to be glanced at

Widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy show the next prayer with a live countdown and turn over exactly at each prayer time. The large widget adds the Hijri date and the Qibla bearing.

Calculated on your iPhone

Times are computed on the device with the open-source Adhan library, so Sukun works offline and on a plane. Twelve calculation methods, Hanafi or standard Asr, and rules for high latitudes such as Northern Europe.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your location is used on the device to calculate times and is never sent to us. Reminders are local notifications. Read the privacy policy.

A compass that knows when you’re aligned

Turn until the needle meets the mark at the top. A soft haptic tells you when you’re facing the Qibla, within three degrees.
